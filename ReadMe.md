# 💫 About Me:
I’m a techie at heart, but for me, technology isn't a grind—it's a playground. While many view coding, scripting, and hardware as just a career path, I dive into technology purely for the joy of creation, discovery, and experimentation.I build things just to see if they can be built. Whether I am automating a mundane daily task, spinning up a home server, or writing a script to solve a ridiculous hyper-specific problem, my main driver is curiosity. I love the thrill of troubleshooting, the satisfaction of a clean line of code, and the freedom of working on projects without deadlines, corporate constraints, or commercial pressure.Ultimately, I am a digital craftsman. I break things, fix them, and learn constantly, all because exploring the digital world is exactly how I choose to have fun.
